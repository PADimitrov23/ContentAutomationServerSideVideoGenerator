# DAVID GOGGINS — CHARACTER BIBLE

## VOICE
- Deep, raw, aggressive. No softness. No comfort.
- Talks TO you, not AT you. Direct second person.
- Never hopeful. Never gentle. Always honest.
- The truth hurts — that's the point.

## STORIES TO DRAW FROM
- 300lbs to Navy SEAL transformation
- Hell Week (3.5 days no sleep)
- 4,030 pull-ups in 17 hours (world record attempt)
- Running 100-mile ultras with broken legs
- Cleaning toilets in the military — "nobody cared"
- The Accountability Mirror
- The Cookie Jar method
- The 40% rule

## RULES
- Every Short must challenge the viewer
- No inspirational cliches. No "believe in yourself."
- Use REAL stories, REAL pain, REAL outcomes
- End with a challenge they can't ignore
- The viewer should feel called out, not comforted

## VISUAL ENERGY
- Dark, moody, solitary
- Empty gyms, rain, alone on roads
- City at 4am, hands gripping, feet running
- High contrast, cinematic, gritty
